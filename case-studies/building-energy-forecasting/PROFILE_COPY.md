## Case-study blurb

Built a virtual-sensor forecasting system that used only two months of target-building data to estimate the remaining roughly 365-day period. The solution combined cross-building transfer learning, SHAP interpretability, blind testing, and a fixed 12-member LightGBM/XGBoost/temporal-profile ensemble. The public headline result was 12.42% MAPE with a 0.9456 correlation coefficient over 7,344 hourly observations.
